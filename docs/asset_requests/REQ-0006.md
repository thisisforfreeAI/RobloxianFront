## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw a single top-down game sprite of a military headquarters compound, seen from directly above with no
> perspective. Canvas 960 × 960 pixels, fully transparent background, PNG. The compound is square and fills
> about 880 × 880 px, centred, with at least 24 px of empty space on every edge: a large flat-roofed command
> building in the middle with a radio mast and a helipad H on the roof, sandbag walls along the outside, a
> gate in the middle of the bottom edge, and a few parked crates and a small flag (plain, no emblem) inside.
> Concrete greys and olive canvas. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow outside the
> compound, no grass or ground outside the walls, no text, no logo, no border. Clear shapes that stay readable
> when shrunk to 240 × 240.

4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
