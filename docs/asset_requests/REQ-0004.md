## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw two separate top-down game sprites of the same main battle tank, seen from directly above with no
> perspective, both pointing to the RIGHT, each on its own 480 × 480 pixel canvas with a fully transparent
> background, PNG.
> Image 1, the HULL without its turret: a tracked hull about 250 px long and 150 px wide, centred in the
> canvas, with the two tracks along the long sides, an engine deck at the left (rear) and an empty round
> turret ring exactly at the centre of the canvas.
> Image 2, the TURRET only, with nothing else: a turret about 120 px across whose turning centre is exactly
> at the centre of the canvas, and a gun barrel pointing right, about 150 px long from the centre.
> When image 2 is laid exactly over image 1 they must form one complete tank. Same olive-green paint on both,
> darker tracks, a small hatch on the turret. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow, no ground,
> no text, no logo, no border. Leave at least 16 px of empty space on every edge of both canvases. Crisp edges
> that stay readable when shrunk to 120 × 120.

## Checks Claude runs on delivery

1. Both 480 × 480, RGBA, transparent, empty 16 px border, facing right; the hull's ring and the turret's centre within 4 px of the image centre; the two images line up when stacked.
2. `rotate_frames.py` on each image (`--id unit_tank_hull` and `--id unit_tank_turret`, `--rev 1`). The game turns the turret layer on its own, then `pack_atlas.py` and `validate_atlas.py`.
3. Studio: it shows on the atlas test board the right way round.
4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
