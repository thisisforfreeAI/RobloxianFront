## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw a single top-down game sprite of a military oil tanker truck (a cab at the front and a long round
> fuel tank behind it on six wheels), seen from directly above with no perspective. The truck points to the
> RIGHT. Canvas 480 × 480 pixels, fully transparent background, PNG. Centre the truck exactly in the canvas;
> it should be about 260 px long and 110 px wide, leaving at least 16 px of empty space on every edge.
> Olive-grey cab with a visible windscreen at the front, a dull steel-grey tank with a hatch on top and a
> thin walkway along it, dark tyres just showing at the sides. Soft even lighting from directly above, no
> cast shadow, no ground, no text, no logo, no border. Crisp edges that stay readable when shrunk to 120 × 120.

## Checks Claude runs on delivery

1. 480 × 480, RGBA, transparent, empty 16 px border, truck centred (alpha centroid within 4 px), facing right.
2. `rotate_frames.py <png> --id unit_oil_truck --rev 1`, then `pack_atlas.py` and `validate_atlas.py`.
3. Studio: it shows on the atlas test board the right way round.
4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
