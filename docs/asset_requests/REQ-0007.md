## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw a single top-down game sprite of a small oil refinery, seen from directly above with no perspective.
> Canvas 576 × 576 pixels, fully transparent background, PNG. The refinery is square and fills about 520 × 520
> px, centred, with at least 16 px of empty space on every edge: two round storage tanks seen from above on
> the left, a tall distillation column (a circle with a ring of walkway) at the top right, a tangle of pipes
> joining them, and a concrete pad with a truck bay at the bottom edge. Steel greys, rust accents and a little
> yellow safety paint. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow, no ground outside the pad, no
> text, no logo, no border. Clear shapes that stay readable when shrunk to 144 × 144.
