## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw a single top-down game sprite of a small infantry squad: four soldiers standing close together in a
> loose diamond, all facing to the RIGHT, seen from directly above with no perspective (you see helmets and
> shoulders, rifles pointing right). Canvas 192 × 192 pixels, fully transparent background, PNG. Centre the
> group exactly in the canvas; the whole group should fit in about 120 × 120 px, leaving at least 8 px of empty
> space on every edge. Olive-green uniforms and helmets, dark rifles, a slightly lighter helmet on the squad
> leader at the front. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow, no ground, no text, no logo, no
> border. Bold, simple shapes that stay readable when shrunk to 48 × 48.

## Checks Claude runs on delivery

1. 192 × 192, RGBA, transparent, empty 8 px border, group centred (alpha centroid within 2 px), facing right.
2. `rotate_frames.py <png> --id unit_infantry_squad --rev 1 --frame 48`, then `pack_atlas.py` and `validate_atlas.py`.
3. Studio: it shows on the atlas test board the right way round.
4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
