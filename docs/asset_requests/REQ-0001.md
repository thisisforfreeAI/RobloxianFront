# REQ-0001: Scout car (pilot)

Pilot request to prove the ChatGPT → pipeline → Studio path with one small, real asset.
Chosen because every unit needs new art while reuse of the original art is unresolved, and the scout car
is the simplest vehicle: wheeled, no turret, one frame.

## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw a single top-down game sprite of a light military scout car (a small four-wheeled open-top recon
> vehicle), seen from directly above with no perspective. The car points to the RIGHT. Canvas 480 × 480
> pixels, fully transparent background, PNG. Centre the car exactly in the canvas; it should be about
> 220 px long and 120 px wide, leaving at least 16 px of empty space on every edge. Neutral olive-grey
> paint with darker tyres, a visible windscreen near the front and a small mounted machine gun at the
> centre. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow, no ground, no text, no logo, no border.
> Crisp edges that stay readable when shrunk to 120 × 120.

## Checks Claude runs on delivery

1. 480 × 480, RGBA, transparent background, empty 16 px border, car centred (alpha centroid within 4 px of the centre), facing right.
2. `rotate_frames.py` → 16 frames, `pack_atlas.py` → 960 × 240 page, `validate_atlas.py` passes.
3. Studio: the car replaces the arrow on the atlas test board and faces each direction correctly.
4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
