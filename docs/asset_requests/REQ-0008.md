## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw six separate top-down game sprites of small town buildings, seen from directly above with no
> perspective (you see only the roofs). Each on its own 384 × 384 pixel canvas with a fully transparent
> background, PNG, centred, with at least 12 px of empty space on every edge:
> 1. a small house with a red tiled pitched roof (ridge running left to right),
> 2. a larger house with a grey slate L-shaped roof,
> 3. a flat-roofed shop with a few vents and an air-conditioning box,
> 4. a barn with a corrugated metal roof,
> 5. a two-storey block with a flat roof, a water tank and a stair hatch,
> 6. a church-like building with a long roof and a small square tower at one end.
> Muted European colours, the same scale for all six. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow,
> no ground, gardens or roads, no text, no logo, no border. Clear shapes that stay readable when shrunk to
> 96 × 96.

## Checks Claude runs on delivery

1. Six files, each 384 × 384, RGBA, transparent, empty 12 px border, centred, same scale.
2. none (no rotate); pack the six frames together, then `pack_atlas.py` and `validate_atlas.py`.
3. Studio: it shows on the atlas test board the right way round.
4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
