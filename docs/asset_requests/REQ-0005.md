## Text to give ChatGPT

> Draw a single top-down game sprite of a self-propelled artillery gun (a tracked vehicle with a large
> boxy gun housing at the rear half and a very long gun barrel), seen from directly above with no perspective.
> The gun points to the RIGHT. Canvas 480 × 480 pixels, fully transparent background, PNG. Centre the vehicle
> exactly in the canvas; including the barrel it should be about 300 px long and 140 px wide, leaving at least
> 16 px of empty space on every edge. Sand-and-olive paint, darker tracks along both sides, a muzzle brake at
> the barrel's tip. Soft even lighting from directly above, no cast shadow, no ground, no text, no logo, no
> border. Crisp edges that stay readable when shrunk to 120 × 120.

## Checks Claude runs on delivery

1. 480 × 480, RGBA, transparent, empty 16 px border, centred (alpha centroid within 4 px of the centre; the long barrel may pull it right by up to 12 px), facing right.
2. `rotate_frames.py <png> --id unit_artillery --rev 1`, then `pack_atlas.py` and `validate_atlas.py`.
3. Studio: it shows on the atlas test board the right way round.
4. Then you approve or ask for a revision (r2).
